# Surface Brief: Pennywise Application UI

## Scope & Mode
- Scope: Complete application UI rewrite (Dashboard, Transactions, Bill Splitter, Budgets & Allowance, Settings/Security)
- Mode: Operate

## Audience & Job
- Indian college students & young adults managing irregular stipends/allowances, campus spends, and flatmate bill splits.
- Needs to know safe spend today, log transactions in seconds via UPI/SMS, and settle debts without paisa leakage.

## Direction contract

THESIS: High-density utilitarian manifest that treats personal accounting as an exact, unpadded operational ledger, refusing the bloated card-with-gradient tropes of standard fintech apps.

OWN-WORLD: Deep midnight enamel shell (#0b0f17, #111827) with high-density tabular typography, ruled hairline borders (#1f293d), amber warning indicators (#f59e0b) for budget thresholds, and emerald verified states (#10b981).

STORY: The student immediately sees unencumbered liquid cash and their safe daily spending limit, pastes or selects common campus transactions in one tap, and tracks roommate IOUs with zero cognitive friction.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile viewport (390px): compact operational header with locked-local status, unencumbered cash balance hero, daily spending allowance gauge with live digit transitions, quick SMS/UPI parse pill, and dense transaction manifest feed.

FORM: The Utilitarian Manifest (position 5 on grounded list); seed:417397cd54b2c089.
